## About

DarkBound is an atmospheric horror RPG that plunges players into a nightmarish world between reality and darkness. Confront terrifying beings, evolve your character, and make decisions that will determine your fate in this forgotten realm.

## Story

You find yourself trapped in a world between reality and nightmare, the darkness. As you navigate through this forgotten realm, you'll encounter terrifying beings and uncover the dark history of this city. Every decision you make will shape your journey and determine your fate.

Choose your class and dive into the dark-bounded city of Kuala Lumpur, traverse through multiple LRT stations lurking with unique enemies and encounters. Level up your character, gain new skills and defeat the evil nestled within the heart of the city
## Getting Started

### Installation

1. Download the latest release from [itch.io](https://hazzyxz.itch.io/darkboundrpg)
2. Extract the ZIP file to your desired location
3. Run `DarkBound.exe` to start the game

## How to Play

### Controls

| Key        | Action              |
| ---------- | ------------------- |
| Arrow Keys | Move your character |
| F          | Interact/Confirm    |
| P          | Save game           |
| ESC        | Save & Exit game    |
*Note: Other key binds are shown on screen in the gameplay. Above are the basics only*
### Tips for Survival

- Save your game frequently
- Manage your MP carefully
- Pay attention to special enemy variations
- Sometimes fleeing is better than fighting
